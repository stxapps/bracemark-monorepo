# bracemark-expo 1.4.1

## App Store

```text
Links to YouTube now get their title and preview image.

• Update preview, and sharing from Safari, now take the YouTube video you’re watching, not the first one you opened.
• If another device signs this one out, the sign-in screen now says so more clearly.
• Fixed: a title with “>” in it was cut short there.
• Fixed: searching in the List or Tags picker after scrolling down hid the top matches.
• Fixed: on a narrow iPhone, or with larger text, the Keep a copy and Update preview buttons were squeezed. They now sit one above the other.

Found a bug, or need help? bracemark.com/support
```

## Google Play
