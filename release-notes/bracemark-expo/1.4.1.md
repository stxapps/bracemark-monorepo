# bracemark-expo 1.4.1

## App Store

The first App Store update after 1.0.0, so it covers everything from 1.1.0
to 1.4.1.

```text
Bracemark now runs on iPad, and in every orientation on iPhone and iPad.

• Keep a copy of a page in read mode, and read it after the page changes or disappears. Tap Read in a link’s menu, then Keep a copy, or keep one as you share from Safari. Part of a Bracemark subscription.
• Creating an account now takes one step.
• Faster sync, with a progress bar on the first sync of a large library.
• Long lists of links scroll more smoothly.
• Links to YouTube now get their title and preview image.
• Fixed: “Sync failed” after coming back to the app.

Found a bug, or need help? bracemark.com/support
```

## Google Play

```text
Links to YouTube now get their title and preview image.

• Update preview now takes the YouTube video you’re watching, not the first one you opened.
• The app no longer has access to Wi-Fi details, which it never used.
• Fixed: a title with “>” in it was cut short there.
• Fixed: searching in the List or Tags picker after scrolling down hid the top matches.
• Fixed: on a narrow phone, the Keep a copy and Update preview buttons were squeezed.

Found a bug, or need help? bracemark.com/support
```
