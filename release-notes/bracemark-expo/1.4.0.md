# bracemark-expo 1.4.0

## App Store

```text
Keep a copy of a page in read mode, and read it after the page changes or disappears.

• Tap Read in a link’s menu, then Keep a copy. Or keep one as you share from Safari.
• Copies kept with the Bracemark browser extension open here too.
• Part of a Bracemark subscription.
• Faster with hundreds of lists and tags.
• Fixed: “Sync failed” after coming back to the app.

Found a bug, or need help? bracemark.com/support
```
