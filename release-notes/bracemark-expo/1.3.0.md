# bracemark-expo 1.3.0

## App Store

```text
Creating an account now takes one step: choose a password, and your account is ready.

• Then a short welcome walks you through setting up a recovery code, checking that your saved password works, and importing the bookmarks you already have. You can skip any step.
• Your password manager can offer to save your password as soon as the account is created.
• Passwords and recovery codes now show in a monospaced font, so characters like 0 and O are easier to tell apart.
• Fixed: if the connection dropped while you changed your password or recovery code, the app told you to try again, even though the change might already have been saved. It now says what may have happened.

Found a bug, or need help? bracemark.com/support
```
