# bracemark-extension 1.3.0

## Firefox Add-ons

```text
Keep a copy of the page you save: its article in read mode, or a screenshot.

- A save keeps a read-mode copy by default. Change this in Settings.
- After a save, click Read mode or Screenshot in the popup to keep one now.
- Read your copies in the Bracemark web app.
- Part of a Bracemark subscription. No new permissions.

Found a bug, or need help? bracemark.com/support
```
