You can use Bracemark in a browser, on an iPhone or Android phone, and through a browser extension for Chrome or Firefox. The native apps are made for phones; the <AppLink path="/">web app</AppLink> also works on tablets.

## Choose how you want to save

- **Browser:** open the <AppLink path="/">web app</AppLink>, sign in, and use Add link. It works without a browser extension.
- **Chrome or Firefox:** install the browser extension from the <StoreLink store="chrome">Chrome Web Store</StoreLink> or <StoreLink store="firefox">Firefox Add-ons</StoreLink>. Sign in inside its popup, even if you are already signed in to the web app. It reads the tab you are saving and can fill in a title and image from that page.
- **iPhone or Android:** install the app from the <StoreLink store="ios">App Store</StoreLink> or <StoreLink store="android">Google Play</StoreLink>. You can add a URL in the app or share a link to it from another app.

The iPhone app requires iOS 26 or later, and the Android app requires Android 13 or later. On an iPad, the iPhone app opens in a phone-sized window. On an Android tablet or Chromebook, use the web app.

Sign in with the same username and password on each device. Each device keeps its own local copy and syncs encrypted changes when connected. See [sync and devices](/docs/sync-and-devices) for what happens offline.
