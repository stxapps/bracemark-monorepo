Sign out removes the account’s local data and session from this browser or app. Your encrypted files remain on your account, ready for you to sign in again on a device with your password or recovery code.

## Sign out here

Open the ⋯ menu above your links and choose Sign out. In a browser, do this separately in the web app and in the browser extension if you use both: each has its own session. On a shared device, close any exported files you downloaded too; sign-out does not delete copies you saved outside the app.

Unsynced changes are stored on this device until they can be sent. Before signing out, check the sync status and connect to the network so those changes reach your other devices. A local copy that is wiped cannot send them later.

## Sign out elsewhere

Settings → Account has Sign out other devices. That revokes their sessions and keeps this device signed in. A device already holding downloaded data may still have that data; session revocation does not erase a copy you no longer control. Changing your password also signs out other devices. See [sync and devices](/docs/sync-and-devices) and [lost password](/docs/lost-password).
