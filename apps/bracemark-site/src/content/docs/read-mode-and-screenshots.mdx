A copy keeps what a page held when you saved it, so you can still see it after the page changes, moves, or goes behind a sign-in. There are two kinds. A read-mode copy is the page’s article: its text, without the ads and menus, and its images if you choose. A screenshot is the part of the page that was in view when you saved it, not all of it.

Keeping a copy is a <PlanName plan="plus" /> feature. Copies you have already kept stay readable on every plan.

## Keep a copy as you save

**Browser extension:** a save keeps a read-mode copy from the open tab. To change what it keeps, open the browser extension’s Settings from the gear in its toolbar popup and go to Keep a copy. There you can turn on Keep a screenshot when you save, or turn off Keep a read-mode copy when you save. After you save, the saved screen also has Read mode and Screenshot, to keep either one then.

**iPhone and iPad:** share a page from Safari to Bracemark and it keeps a read-mode copy, taken from the page as you see it. To stop that, turn off Keep a read-mode copy when you share from Safari in Settings → Read mode. A share from another app, or from Android, keeps the link but no copy; keep one later from the app, below.

A page that isn’t an article, such as a product page or a dashboard, has no read-mode copy to keep. A screenshot from the browser extension is the copy for those.

## Keep a copy later

**In the iPhone or Android app:** open the link’s menu and tap Read. On a link with no copy, the page opens inside the app. Tap Keep a copy under it. If the page needs you to sign in, sign in on that screen; the app stays signed in to that site. If the page jumps to another address as it loads, the buttons stay off and no copy can be kept from there.

**In the web app:** open the link’s menu and click Read, then Keep a read-mode copy. A browser can’t open other sites for the web app, so this copy comes from [our link-extraction service](/extractor), once you turn on Server read mode. You can turn it on there or in <AppLink path="/settings/read-mode">Settings → Read mode</AppLink>. It fetches the page only when you ask, one link at a time. It receives the link’s address, not your account, and briefly holds your IP address. It can’t sign in for you, so a page behind a login gives it no article. It also keeps none from a site that asks services like ours not to copy its pages. For those, open the page and save it with the browser extension.

Only the browser extension takes screenshots. The phone apps and the web app keep read-mode copies only.

## Images in a copy

A read-mode copy keeps its text only, unless you turn on Include images in read-mode copies. The setting is in Settings → Read mode in the phone apps, in the web app once Server read mode is on, and in the browser extension’s Settings. Where you keep a copy, Include images also sits under the button. It’s one setting for your whole account. Images use far more storage than text. Turning it on or off doesn’t change copies you have already kept.

## Read a copy

Open the link’s menu and choose Read. A link with both kinds of copy shows a tab for each, Read mode and Screenshot. Click or tap the screenshot to see it at full size.

Each copy is encrypted on your device before it syncs, like your links. It downloads to a device the first time you open it there, so open it once while you’re online. Search doesn’t look inside a copy.

## When the page is gone

Most links have no copy: links you imported, links saved on <PlanName plan="free" />, and links saved before your plan included copies. For those, and for a page that has changed since, the reader’s ⋯ menu has Open in Wayback Machine. It opens the Internet Archive’s capture of that address nearest the day you saved the link. The app sends the archive nothing until you choose it. If the archive has no capture, its own page says so.

## Delete a copy

Open the copy and choose Delete this read-mode copy or Delete this screenshot, below it. The copy is deleted from all your devices, and the link stays. Keep a copy again and it is taken from the page as it is then, which may not match the one you deleted.

To delete the copies of several links at once, [select them](/docs/select-and-bulk-edit) in the web app and choose Delete copies. Deleting a link permanently deletes its copies too.

## Storage, plans, and export

Copies count towards your account’s storage. On a paid plan, Settings → Subscription shows how much your library uses. If your plan ends, your copies stay readable, and you can still export or delete them. Only keeping new ones stops. See [manage your subscription](/docs/manage-your-subscription).

A Bracemark backup includes every copy. HTML bookmarks, CSV, and plain text don’t include any. See [export your data](/docs/export).
