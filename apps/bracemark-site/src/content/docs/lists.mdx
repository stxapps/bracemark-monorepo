Every link belongs to one list. My List is the starting place; Archive and Trash have their own jobs. Use a list when you want a link to live in one place, and [tags](/docs/tags-and-notes) when it belongs to several subjects.

## Make and use a list

Open Settings → Lists to create, rename, reorder, or delete lists. You can also create a list while choosing where to save a link. Choose a list in Add link or Edit link, or use Move to in the link’s menu.

Lists can nest inside other lists on <PlanName plan="plus" />. Flat lists work on <PlanName plan="free" />. Imported browser folders keep their hierarchy on every plan, so an import does not discard your folder structure. See [import your links](/docs/import).

Archive moves a link out of your working list while keeping it saved. Show All shows links across lists you can currently open. Removing a link puts it in Trash; [restore](/docs/restore-a-link) brings it back to My List.

A list lock is a separate screen lock over its contents. You can set one in Settings → Lists; [app and list locks](/docs/app-lock-and-list-locks) explains its limits.
