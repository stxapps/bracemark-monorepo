A saved link starts with its address. You can add a title of your own, put it in one list, give it several tags, and write a note. You can pin it, archive it, or move it to Trash without changing the original address.

## Your details and fetched details

The title you write is yours. A preview may supply a page title and image when one is available, but it doesn’t replace your title. These fetched details are kept separately from the fields you edit, so a preview arriving later doesn’t overwrite your note or tags. [Link previews](/docs/link-previews) explains the different fetch paths and why some pages provide no details.

<Figure name="edit-link" />

The saved link is a pointer to a live page. The local copy lets you see its address, title, tags, note, and preview while offline. Opening the live page still needs a connection.

For a portable copy, [export](/docs/export) as a Bracemark backup. HTML bookmarks, CSV, and plain text leave out different fields; choose the format for where the links are going.
