Open Settings → Subscription to see the plan on your account, its renewal or end date, and where you bought it. Your plan applies across devices, but billing stays with the provider that sold it.

## Manage or cancel

If you subscribed on the web, open <AppLink path="/settings/subscription">Settings → Subscription</AppLink> and choose Manage billing. Paddle’s portal handles payment methods, invoices, and cancellation. If you subscribed in the iPhone or Android app, choose Manage subscription in that app’s settings; it opens the App Store or Play Store subscription controls. A store purchase shown on another platform still needs to be managed in the store that sold it.

Cancelling stops renewal; the current paid period remains until its shown end. When it ends, your links remain readable, searchable, editable, and exportable. If you are above the <PlanName plan="free" /> link limit, adding new links stops until you delete links permanently or subscribe again. Moving a link to Trash does not free a place under the limit. Existing nested lists and locks remain usable; you can remove a lock or flatten a list. See [pricing](/pricing) for current plans and [export](/docs/export) for a copy you can keep.

## A purchase has not appeared

Wait a minute, then use Refresh in Settings → Subscription. In the mobile app, Restore purchases checks purchases held by that device’s store. If the plan still does not appear, email [support@bracemark.com](mailto:support@bracemark.com) with the purchase date and the store or web checkout you used. Do not buy the same plan again while the first purchase is being checked.
