App and list locks put a second gate over content on a signed-in device. They are <PlanName plan="plus" /> features. Your account encryption still protects what is synced; the lock controls what someone can see while the app is open.

## Set a lock

Open Settings → Misc to set or remove the app lock. Open Settings → Lists and use a list’s ⋯ menu to lock that list. A list lock can also hide its name from the sidebar. Use Lock app in the menu above your links, or Lock now beside a list, when you want to close it immediately.

The iPhone and Android apps can use Face ID, Touch ID, or the device’s biometric unlock where available. The web app uses the lock password. A list’s contents stay out of browse, search, and export while it is locked; unlock it before making a complete [export](/docs/export).

There is no recovery code for a forgotten lock password. Signing out clears local locks; sign back in with your account password or recovery code. Sign-out also clears this device’s local data, so [let changes sync](/docs/sync-and-devices) first. A lock password is separate from your account password and doesn’t replace it.
