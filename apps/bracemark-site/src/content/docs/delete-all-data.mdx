Delete all data empties your library on every device and keeps your account, so you can still sign in and start again. To close the account too, see [delete your account](/delete-account) instead; it deletes the library with it.

## Before you start

- **Export anything you want to keep.** Deleting can’t be undone. A [Bracemark backup](/docs/export) can be imported again later.
- **Let your other devices sync.** If another device has changes that haven’t synced yet, those changes may sync back afterwards. Open Bracemark on each device and let it finish syncing first. Changes this device hasn’t synced are deleted along with everything else.

## Delete it

**In a browser:** open <AppLink path="/settings/data">Settings → Data</AppLink> and click Delete all data. Tick “Yes, I’m absolutely sure I want to delete all my data.” and click Delete all data.

**In the Android or iOS app:** tap the ⋯ menu above your links, then Settings. Tap Data, then Delete all data. Tick the box to confirm and tap Delete all data.

If a sync is running, Bracemark waits for it to finish before it deletes anything, which can take a minute. When it’s done, it shows how many items were deleted. If it fails, it says so; try again. The message says whether this device’s unsynced changes were kept.

## What is deleted

- Every saved link in every list, all your lists and tags, and all your settings.
- The files that go with them: preview images, images you set yourself, and [read-mode copies and screenshots](/docs/read-mode-and-screenshots).
- These go from the storage your devices sync with straight away. Each of your other devices empties its own copy the next time it syncs.

Our server backups still hold the deleted files until they expire. The [privacy policy](/privacy) says how long that is.

## What stays

Your account stays as it was: your username, your password and recovery code, and your subscription. This device stays signed in, and so do your others. To sign a device out as well, see [sign out](/docs/sign-out).
