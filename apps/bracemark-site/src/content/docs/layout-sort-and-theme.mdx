How your links are laid out, the order they come in, and the app’s colours are all in one place. In a browser, open <AppLink path="/settings/misc">Settings → Misc.</AppLink> In the Android or iOS app, tap the ⋯ menu above your links, then Settings, then Misc.

## Layout and order

**Link layout** is “List”, a dense single column, or “Card”, a grid of preview cards.

**Link sort** orders your links by “Date added”, when you saved each one, or by “Date modified”, when you last changed it. Order is “Newest first” or “Oldest first”. The sort applies on all your devices.

## Theme

**Theme** is “Light”, “Dark”, or “System”, which follows your device’s appearance. “Custom time” switches between the two by the clock: set Light starts and Dark starts.

## The same on every device, or not

Link layout and Theme each have two tabs, “Sync” and “Device”. “Sync” applies your choice on all your devices. “Device” keeps a separate choice for this device only — cards on a laptop and a list on your phone, for example. A “Device” choice is cleared when you sign out. See [sync and devices](/docs/sync-and-devices).
