How your links are laid out, what each one shows, the order they come in, and the app’s colours are all in one place. In a browser, open <AppLink path="/settings/appearance">Settings → Appearance</AppLink>. In the Android or iOS app, tap the ⋯ menu above your links, then Settings, then Appearance.

## Layout and order

**Link layout** is “List”, a dense single column, or “Card”, a grid of preview cards. In the web app, it can also be “Table”: rows of links with the columns you choose.

“Table” is a <PlanName plan="plus" /> feature. The list and card layouts, and all their options, stay free. The table needs a wide window. On a narrow one, your links show as a list until the window is wider. The title is always the first column. Under the layout, choose the other columns and put them in order. Click a date column’s header to sort by that date, and click it again to flip the order. If the window is too narrow for every column you chose, the least important ones are hidden, and “+N” in the header shows which.

Under the layout are its options. Each layout keeps its own. “Title lines” and “Tag lines” set the most lines a title or its tags can use. A card also has a size and an image height. You can choose what each link shows: the site or its full URL, a date, its list name, its tags, and icons for a note or a kept copy.

**Link sort** orders your links by “Date added”, when you saved each one, or by “Date modified”, when you last changed it. Order is “Newest first” or “Oldest first”.

## View options

View options changes the layout, its options, and the sort while your links stay in view. In a browser, click the sliders button above your links. On a narrow window, it is in the ⋯ menu. In the Android or iOS app, tap the ⋯ menu above your links, then View options. It changes the choice that is in use, “Sync” or “Device”, and says which.

## Theme

**Theme** is “Light”, “Dark”, or “System”, which follows your device’s appearance. “Custom time” switches between the two by the clock: set Light starts and Dark starts.

## The same on every device, or not

Link layout and Theme each have two tabs, “Sync” and “Device”. “Sync” applies your choice on all your devices. “Device” keeps a separate choice for this device only — cards on a laptop and a list on your phone, for example. The Link layout tabs cover its options and the sort too. A “Device” choice is cleared when you sign out. See [sync and devices](/docs/sync-and-devices).
