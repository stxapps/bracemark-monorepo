## Save a link, then find it again

Bracemark is an end-to-end encrypted bookmark manager. You can save a link from the web app, the iOS or Android app, or the Chrome or Firefox browser extension. Give it a title, put it in a list, and add tags or a note when they help you find it later. [Get the apps](/docs/get-the-apps) shows where each one fits.

The app keeps a local copy so you can search the links already on your device while offline. When you reconnect, changes sync to your other devices. [Sync and devices](/docs/sync-and-devices) explains what happens when two devices edit the same link.

## What reaches our servers

Your links, lists, tags, and notes are encrypted on your device before they sync. Our account service holds the details needed to sign you in and run sync, including your username and sessions. It cannot read your saved links. If you choose server previews in the web app, our separate preview service receives a link’s address so it can fetch that page’s title or image. [Security](/security) and [link previews](/docs/link-previews) explain those boundaries in full.

That design has costs. Search runs on the copy your device has, so a new device needs to sync before it can find links saved elsewhere. There are no shared lists. Save your recovery code: if you lose both it and your password, we cannot reset the account for you. [How encryption and search work](/docs/how-encryption-and-search-work) walks through the data path and the tradeoffs.

## Bring your links, and keep a way out

You can [import links](/docs/import) from a browser bookmarks file, another supported export, or a Bracemark backup. Before you change the old collection, check a few imported lists and links. Every plan can [export](/docs/export) a readable Bracemark backup or a format another app can use. An export is a separate copy you control; it is not the live sync path.

You can start with the free plan. [Get the apps](/docs/get-the-apps) has the entry points, and [pricing](/pricing) has the current plan details.
